# Assembly method — Drop-Dropper

## 1. Actuator sub-assembly
1. Epoxy the piezo stack (10 x 10 x 20 mm) into the mount-block pocket with its rear face
   against the pocket back wall. Let the adhesive cure fully.
2. Epoxy-bond the steel end cap (with M3 stud) to the piezo front face, stud pointing +X.
3. Slide the preload spring over the stud area behind the mount (or between a rear stop and
   the rod coupler) so it pushes the rod assembly toward the piezo with ~5–10 N.

## 2. Rod and clamp
4. Screw the coupler nut onto the M3 stud, then screw the steel rod (M3 threaded end) into
   the nut from the other side; tighten both locknuts against each other. The rod must move
   freely in X but with no play.
5. Bolt the clamp lower jaw to the rod tip collar (2x M3). Stem groove channel vertical.
6. Fit silicone pads into both V-grooves. Mount the lever jaw on the Ø4 pivot pin with the
   return spring arranged to keep it open; pressing the lever with two fingers closes the
   clamp around the stem.

## 3. Installation
7. Bolt the base plate to the bench (4x M4) or to a heavy lab-stand post — the mount must be
   rigid so vibration goes into the plant, not the bench.
8. Place the weighing dish on the bench directly under the rosette position.
9. Connect the HV amplifier output to the piezo leads (insulated terminals, power off).

## 4. Operation
10. Clamp the stem (<= 1 mm) gently with the lever — two-finger close, do not over-squeeze.
11. Set function generator to ~1 kHz sine, amplifier gain low; increase amplitude until
    droplets visibly shed into the dish. Sweep frequency 500 Hz–2 kHz to find the optimum.
12. Harvest the dish contents (pipette / weigh) after each run. Power off HV before
    touching the plant or wiring.
